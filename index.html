Problem solving and innovation

Developed an MS Excel VBA Macro-based automation solution to simplify and improve the POS merchant transaction settlement process at Nepal SBI Bank.

Problem
Previously, POS merchant transaction settlement was performed manually. The process was time-consuming, taking approximately 5–6 hours to complete. Manual calculations and data handling also increased the risk of errors, affecting the efficiency and reliability of the settlement process.
Approach
Due to the high cost of purchasing a dedicated settlement application, management decided not to proceed with the proposed software solution. To address this challenge, I independently developed an MS Excel VBA Macro-based automation tool to streamline and automate the settlement process without requiring additional software investment.
Result
The automation reduced the POS merchant transaction settlement time from approximately 5–6 hours to less than one hour. It significantly minimized manual work and the possibility of human errors while improving overall operational efficiency and settlement accuracy. Additionally, it provided a cost-effective solution by utilizing existing MS Excel resources without requiring investment in expensive third-party software.
Next step
Develop a centralized server-based database and application to further enhance the automation process, improve data management, and support scalability, security, and reporting.
